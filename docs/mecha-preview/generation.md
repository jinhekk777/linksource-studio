The colossal physical weight and scale of giant robot cinema, without using recognizable existing film characters or named robots. A single striking original graphite-armored mech, broad shoulders, architectural layered steel plates, restrained periwinkle energy slits, emerging through ocean mist behind small cranes and distant buildings. Main mech's head and torso are in the upper center-right (about 60% horizontal), leaving darker quieter lower-left space for website title overlay. Wide 16:9 composition suitable for fullscreen landscape and a center crop on mobile. Sophisticated AAA concept-art realism, atmospheric volumetric lighting, crisp silhouette, physically plausible rain and metal, subtle film grain, deep charcoal and desaturated navy with restrained lavender #7d8bff highlights, a few warm industrial light accents. High visual impact through scale and composition, not clutter. No text, no lettering, no logo, no UI, no watermark, no humans in close-up, no branded film designs.
